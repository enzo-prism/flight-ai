Capabilities / Release notes

More model choice, clearer operations

OpenRouter expands AI model options, updated defaults reach workflow tools, and usage views become faster and clearer.

Release period: July 18–24, 2026

The customer benefit

Choose from a broader range of models and spend less time checking the status of your operations.

Explore Mach 1 →

Improvement 01

Broader AI model selection

OpenRouter model options are available in the application, with routing support across key evaluation tools, including models such as GPT-5.4 mini.

What this means for your team

Give your team more flexibility in selecting models for supported tasks.

Improvement 02

Updated default models

Default models moved to Gemini 3.6 Flash across workflow chat, schedule generation, bot configurations, and background systems. Settings pointing to older default versions are updated automatically.

What this means for your team

Keep supported workflows on the updated default without manually changing each older setting.

Improvement 03

Faster usage summaries

Optimized backend lookups speed up execution summary loading for active accounts.

What this means for your team

Review activity with less waiting for usage information to load.

Improvement 04

Clearer active execution views

Paused tasks are filtered out of active execution views.

What this means for your team

Help support and operations teams focus on work that is currently active.

Fixes that remove friction

  • Fixed failed cancellation of stuck or stale workflow executions so users can clear those runs reliably.

Behind the experience

  • Unused database tables and system routes were removed to improve maintainability.
  • Regular frontend and Pi agent-engine updates keep platform components synchronized.
