Understand your estimated AI usage costs

Administrators can see estimated AI usage costs by model, review an aggregate total, and include cost insights in CSV exports.

Release period: August 22–28, 2026

The customer benefit

Give budget conversations a clearer starting point with model-level estimates your team can review and export.

Improvement 01

Estimated costs by model

The admin usage dashboard now breaks estimated AI usage costs down by model and shows an aggregate total. These figures are estimates, not invoices.

What this means for your team

Understand which models contribute to estimated usage costs without piecing together separate reports.

Improvement 02

Exportable cost insights

Include estimated AI cost information in downloadable CSV reports.

What this means for your team

Bring the same usage estimates into your existing budget tracking and review process.

Behind the experience

  • Updated database connection components improve reliability and help prevent temporary data access interruptions.
  • A production release brought accumulated platform enhancements and stability improvements to users.
