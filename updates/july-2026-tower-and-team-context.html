Experience / Release notes

Keep Tower close and team knowledge organized

A persistent Tower sidebar keeps assistant sessions in reach, while shared context and secret controls support work across teams.

Release period: July 11–17, 2026

The customer benefit

Keep your place with your assistant and give teams more control over the information and credentials they share.

Explore Mach 1 →

Improvement 01

A persistent, resizable Tower

Tower lives in a resizable right sidebar. Active assistant sessions persist as you navigate, close and reopen panels, or refresh the browser.

What this means for your team

Move between tasks without losing your assistant session.

Improvement 02

Organization and department context

Share and manage context at organization and department levels, and move information between scopes by promoting or demoting it.

What this means for your team

Organize shared knowledge around the teams that need it.

Improvement 03

Shared secret controls

Manage secrets at organization and department levels with granular grant controls and clear safety warnings.

What this means for your team

Give teams explicit controls over access to shared sensitive credentials.

Behind the experience

  • Database updates support workflow settings, shared secrets, and organization-wide context while optimizing queries.
  • Accumulated application updates and underlying provider maintenance improve the platform foundation.
